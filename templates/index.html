Responsible AI & Academic Research Notice

This platform is an educational machine learning analytics demonstration built using a synthetic dataset of student academic patterns (300 records, 70/15/15 stratified split). Predictions are statistical correlations derived by the Feedforward Neural Network and do not establish causal relationships or constitute formal academic counseling advice.
